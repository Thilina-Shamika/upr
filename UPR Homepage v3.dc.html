§ 07 — FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Every structure we produce — PE, PE Plus and PE Plus Xtra — is designed to be recyclable, including our metalised barrier film. The goal is to keep material in the loop rather than sending it to landfill.

PE is standard polyethylene for everyday packaging. PE Plus is a multi-polymer structure with enhanced performance for demanding uses. PE Plus Xtra adds a metalised barrier coating for premium products — all three remain recyclable.

Around 40% of our product is made from recycled plastic, and 22,346,157 KG of sustainable material has gone into production to date. We keep reducing the virgin polymer entering the system.
