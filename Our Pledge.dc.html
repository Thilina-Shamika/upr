01 Our Pledge

Making plastic sustainable

Using plastic responsibly is about the freedom and convenience of plastic products, used responsibly. We pledge to operate and improve the circular economy we have created for plastic.

02 Eco-friendly strategy

A strategy built on four pillars.

Our environmental strategy with plastics is fourfold. We believe a focus on these areas will let us reduce — and one day eliminate — the plastic film pollution problem in Sri Lanka.

We support global concerns on plastic and work unwaveringly towards sustainability through eco-friendly production practices. As the undisputed leader in the processing of recycled plastics, we strive to maintain 50% of group output from recycled plastics obtained from waste material and bio-based plastics — making us partly sustainable, in line with the circular economy of plastic.

01 Pillar

Recycle

We offer a range of products that can be recycled continuously. Approximately 40% of our product is made from recycled plastic.

Our own supply chain covers collection, processing of plastic waste, processing of finished product, and distribution of recycle-based products into local and export markets.

Recycled content ~40% of our product
Supply chain Collection to distribution, in-house
Markets Local and export

Biodegradable Products

We provide customers with guaranteed starch-based, 100% biodegradable and compostable products.

These have become an essential part of our customers' lifestyle — from grow bags to lunch sheets that break down naturally at end of life.
