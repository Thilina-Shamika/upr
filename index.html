08 FAQ

Questions, answered

Every structure we produce — PE, PE Plus and PE Plus Xtra — is designed to be recyclable, including our metalised barrier film. The goal is to keep material in the loop rather than sending it to landfill.

PE is standard polyethylene for everyday packaging. PE Plus is a multi-polymer structure with enhanced performance for demanding uses. PE Plus Xtra adds a metalised barrier coating for premium products — all three remain recyclable.

Approximately 40% of our product is made from recycled plastic, and 22,346,157 KG of sustainable material has gone into our production to date. We are working to further reduce the virgin polymer entering the system.

Get in touch through our Join Us page. Partnering means moving to recycled or biodegradable options — and being recognised on this website as one of our partners in environmental sustainability.
